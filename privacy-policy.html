About this policy

App: Qibla Direction
Package: com.afaquekhan.qibladirection
Effective date: October 15, 2026

Qibla Direction provides a Qibla compass, prayer times, local reminders and nearby mosque search. A Qibla Direction account is not required. Optional premium skins use Google Play purchases; purchasing requires an eligible Google Play account. The app does not display advertising or sell personal information.

1. Location and searches

With your permission, approximate or precise device location is used for Qibla direction, prayer calculations, location labels and nearby mosque results. Device geocoding services may process coordinates or place-search text. Providers can vary by device.

Nearby mosque searches send an approximate search area, rounded to about a 1 km grid, and a radius over HTTPS to the Private.coffee Overpass service. The radius includes a buffer, and exact distances are filtered on your device. As with other internet services, the provider may receive your network IP address. Provider information and privacy: https://overpass.private.coffee/

Choosing an external maps action passes the selected location or search to that maps service. Its privacy practices then apply.

2. Sensors, reminders and audio

Compass, orientation and magnetic-field readings are processed on your device. Qibla Direction does not send these sensor readings to a developer-operated server. Reminder preferences, prayer schedules and selected audio references are stored locally. Notification and alarm access support reminders. Phone audio files you select are played locally and are not uploaded by Qibla Direction. The app does not record microphone audio.

3. Purchases and billing services

Optional skins are one-time, non-consumable purchases through Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Core Qibla and prayer features remain free. Google Play processes payment and account information under its own terms and privacy policy. Qibla Direction does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

Product IDs, purchase receipts or tokens, transaction status and ownership information are used to validate, acknowledge, restore and reconcile purchases. Verified receipts are stored on your device. Google Play and the Unity billing SDK may process transaction information, installation or player identifiers, session identifiers, device/app information, IP address or inferred country, and operational diagnostics for billing, security, fraud prevention and service operation or measurement. The billing SDK can contact its services when the skin store initializes, including before you choose to buy.

Unity In-App Purchasing privacy information: https://docs.unity.com/en-us/iap/privacy-and-consent/overview
Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising, analytics and accounts

Qibla Direction does not display ads or use advertising identifiers for marketing, and does not require you to create an app account. The release sets Unity advertising and analytics consent signals to Denied for services that honor UnityConsent. This does not prevent necessary billing, security or diagnostic processing described in this policy; the billing SDK is not a zero-data service. The app does not enable optional advertising or gameplay analytics collection.

5. Local data, retention and deletion

Location preferences, calculation settings, skin selection, reminder settings, cached mosque results and verified purchase receipts may be stored locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local data. It does not cancel a purchase or delete Google Play or Unity transaction records. A purchased skin can be restored using the Google Play account that owns it.

Qibla Direction does not operate a user-account or personal-profile database. Independent map, platform and billing providers apply their own retention practices and legal obligations. For a request about data processed on our behalf, contact afaque01@gmail.com so we can identify the request and coordinate with the relevant provider where applicable. Requests concerning providers' independent processing can also be made to those providers. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or purchase tokens by email.

6. Security and your choices

App-controlled requests transmitting data use HTTPS/TLS. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. You can manage location and notification access in Android settings, change local app preferences, avoid optional searches or purchases, and use core features without buying a skin. Denying location may limit location-dependent features unless you provide a manual location. Permission changes do not remove records already held by independent providers.

7. Children's privacy

Qibla Direction is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Purchases are subject to Google Play account, payment and parental-control requirements.

8. Changes and contact

We update this policy when app features or data practices change and revise the effective date above. Privacy and app support contact: afaque01@gmail.com.
