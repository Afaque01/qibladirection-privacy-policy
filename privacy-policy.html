Privacy Policy

App: Qibla Direction
Package: com.afaquekhan.qibladirection
Effective date: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qibla Direction accesses, uses, transmits, and stores information when you use the app.

Qibla Direction does not require a user account. The current release does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information.

1. Information the app accesses

Approximate and precise location. If you grant location permission, Qibla Direction uses your device location to calculate Qibla direction, prayer information, location-related display information, and nearby mosque results.

Place and mosque searches. When you type a place or location into a search feature, the search text may be processed by Android's geocoding service or another device-provided geocoding provider in order to resolve that search to a location.

Compass and device sensors. Qibla Direction uses device heading, orientation, and magnetic-field sensor information for compass functionality and calibration guidance. This sensor information is processed on the device and is not sent by Qibla Direction to our servers.

2. How information is used

The information described above is used only to provide app functionality, including:

  • calculating and displaying the Qibla direction;
  • calculating and displaying prayer times;
  • providing compass accuracy and calibration guidance;
  • finding and displaying nearby mosques;
  • resolving user-entered place searches;
  • supporting local prayer reminders and app preferences.

3. Information transmitted to third-party services

Some functionality requires information to leave your device. Google Play may classify these transmissions as collection or sharing.

  • Nearby mosque lookup: approximate or precise coordinates and a search radius may be sent over HTTPS to an OpenStreetMap Overpass service so that nearby mosque data can be returned.
  • Android geocoding: coordinates or user-entered place-search text may be processed by the geocoding provider available on your Android device. The provider can vary by device and Android configuration.
  • External maps: if you choose to open a mosque or location in another maps application, location information is passed to the app you choose. That app's privacy practices apply after it receives the information.

Qibla Direction does not control the independent retention practices of third-party map, geocoding, or platform service providers.

4. Local app data

App preferences, reminder settings, selected calculation settings, and other feature settings may be stored locally on your device. Qibla Direction does not operate a user-account database containing personal profiles.

You can remove app-local data by clearing Qibla Direction's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. Data retention and deletion

Qibla Direction does not maintain a developer-controlled account system or personal-profile database. Because there is no account system, the app does not provide an account-based data deletion request mechanism.

Data stored locally by the app can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Information transmitted to third-party services may be retained by those services according to their own policies.

6. Security

Qibla Direction uses secure HTTPS/TLS connections for app-controlled network requests that transmit user data. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the app is designed to minimize the data transmitted and to use it only for requested functionality.

7. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The current Qibla Direction release does not contain advertising, does not use advertising identifiers for marketing, does not include analytics SDKs, and does not require users to create or sign in to an account.

8. Children's privacy

Qibla Direction is not directed to children under 13. The Google Play target audience for the app is users aged 13 and older.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
